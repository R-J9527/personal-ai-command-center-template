# Shared-center confidentiality boundary

This private repository stores coordination-only task envelopes. It is not a document store and must never receive company files or company-account context.

Allowed: neutral task title/code, assigner, assignee, priority, due/check date, red-yellow-green health, status, next action, blocker, completion criteria, and a short safe evidence statement.

Forbidden: attachments, document contents, customer details, company email or chat text, meeting transcripts, internal URLs, cloud links, local/network paths, source code, credentials, tokens, secrets, and automatic extraction from a company GPT account.

Outbound updates from a company account require the member to review the complete task envelope and explicitly approve publication. Employer policy takes precedence; disable outbound synchronization when even minimal metadata may not leave the company account.
